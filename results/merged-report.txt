================================================================================
MERGE METHODOLOGY
================================================================================
Three benchmark runs were compared. For each operation/framework/dataset:
- Runs with CV% > 50% were excluded as outliers (likely caused by system interference)
- The median of the remaining mean execution times was selected
- This provides a stable central estimate unaffected by single-run anomalies

Stability comparison across 3 runs (number of unstable operations per framework):
  Report 1 (19:46): rawsql=9, prisma=4, typeorm=4, sequelize=5, drizzle=3
  Report 2 (20:02): rawsql=9, prisma=4, typeorm=6, sequelize=6, drizzle=3
  Report 3 (20:19): rawsql=8, prisma=2, typeorm=1, sequelize=2, drizzle=1
  → Report 3 had the most stable conditions overall

Note: At dataset size 100, all runs show high instability due to the
50ms GC pause dominating actual query time. Results at sizes 1000+ are
more reliable.
