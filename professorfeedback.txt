##Professor Reply 2 : 

A few remarks after reading your thesis:

- Please significantly extend the chapter describing the technologies used in the thesis. Now there is only one sentence per technology in chapter 2;.1. It's one of the formal requirements to describe the technologies used in the thesis, justify why you've chosen them, describe their properties and limitations, give the examples etc.
- Node.js and Postgres should also be described.
- Regarding the text structure: Please avoid using short paragraphs. If there is only one or two sentences then it usually shouldn't be a separate paragraph. The text should have more continuous form.
- I think you should add another research question which is important here: Code maintainability. It's sometimes preferred more than performance.
- Please join chapters 4 and 5. They both describe the research methodology.
- Please add ERD for the database description and provide the information about the number of records in each table.
- All of the tests must be precisely described. You can't just write "basic CRUD operations". What operations were performed? On which table? Is it a series of operations or only one?
- For a performance comparison checking the execution time the typical research method is to repeat an experiment about 10 times and calculate the average and to check if the results are stable and are not affected by e.g. buffering, other tasks performed on the computer etc.
